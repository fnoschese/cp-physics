How to use this simulation

  • Balance: press Put car on balance to find the car's mass.
  • Launcher: drag the car in the Launcher close-up to pull the spring back (or use the − / + buttons). Read the compression from the ruler.
  • Velocimeter: drag the velocimeter gate along the track (or use the arrow buttons). Use the close-up ruler to place it exactly. The meterstick's 0 is where the car leaves the launcher.
  • Press Launch. The velocimeter shows the car's speed as it passes through the gate. Like the real lab, results vary a little from trial to trial, so do more than one trial.
